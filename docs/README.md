## Ground rules

1. **One platform.** Every product is a tier/entitlement of the same codebase —
   never a fork per customer or per SKU.
2. **The demo is the product.** Selling anything means provisioning a live,
   branded instance during the sales conversation.
3. **One language per campaign.** Arabic first, then Russian, then Urdu — each
   with its own voice, script, and ads.
4. **Client isolation.** A tenant/user only ever sees their own brand, data,
   and leads. No client is ever mentioned to another.
